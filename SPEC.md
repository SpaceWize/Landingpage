## 5. Components and motion

- **Build method:** HTML, CSS, canvas, and JavaScript. All motion is built in code. No video backgrounds.
- **Layout feel:** mix of arches plus photo grid. Arch-shaped service cards from screenshot 1 with screenshot 2's grid of work photos and faded background words.
- **Motion level:** the user's answer was "it has to make a very good impression and be something that is remembered." Read alongside "must run well on a cheap Android phone," this means a few memorable, cheap-to-run moments rather than constant animation. The specific effects are chosen in /search and need the user's approval there.
- **Performance rules for motion:** animate only `transform` and `opacity`; no scroll-jacking; heavy effects are skipped on low-end devices (`navigator.hardwareConcurrency <= 4` or `deviceMemory <= 4`) and when Save-Data is on.

| Component | Behaviour | Reduced-motion fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Hero headline | Letters or lines reveal on load | Text visible immediately, no animation |
| Ghost words (giant faded section words) | Drift sideways slowly as the page scrolls | Static, fixed position |
| Work photo grid | Tiles fade and rise in as they enter view; tap opens a larger view | Tiles visible immediately; larger view opens without transition |
| Arch service cards | Lift and outline brightens on hover or focus | Outline change only, no movement |
| Reviews | Numbered slider (01/07 style) with arrows and swipe | Same slider, instant slide change, no autoplay |
| Portrait | Circular crop, subtle scale-in on entering view | Static |
| Book Now button | Outline-to-fill on hover or focus | Instant color change |
| Sticky Book Now bar (phones) | Slides in after the hero leaves view | Appears without sliding |
| Map | Loads on tap or when scrolled near, to protect load time | Same |

Every animation is wrapped in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` or checked in JavaScript. Nothing autoplays for longer than 5 seconds without a pause control.
